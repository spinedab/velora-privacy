Privacy Policy - Velora Health

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Velora Health is a local-only Chrome extension that reminds you to rest your eyes, fix your posture and drink water on a schedule you choose. It lives entirely in its toolbar popup and Chrome's alarm and notification APIs. It is designed with a privacy-first architecture.

Data Collection

Velora Health does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on external servers. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no telemetry transmitted outside of the browser.

How Data Is Handled

  • No network requests of any kind. This extension has no host permissions and makes no fetch, XHR, WebSocket or beacon calls. There is no AI, no API key and no server component.
  • No page access whatsoever. The extension has no activeTab, no scripting, no tabs permission and no content scripts. It cannot see what you browse, which sites you visit, or anything on any page. It does not know whether you are working — it only knows the clock and the schedule you set.
  • Local storage only. Your intervals, work-hours window and the log of completed breaks are stored exclusively in chrome.storage.local on the user's device.
  • What the log contains. One entry per break you mark as Done: a timestamp and which reminder it was. Nothing else. It exists so the popup can show a weekly chart and a streak.
  • Notifications are local. Reminders are produced by Chrome's own notification system on your device. Nothing about them is reported anywhere.
  • Export is manual. The CSV of your log is produced only when you click the export link, and it is generated in the popup itself — the extension does not even request the downloads permission.
  • No user accounts. The extension does not require or support user accounts, login, or registration of any kind.

Permissions Explained

  • alarms - schedules your reminders. A service worker cannot keep a timer alive, so Chrome's alarm system is what wakes the extension at the right moment.
  • notifications - shows the reminder, with its Done and Snooze buttons.
  • storage - saves your intervals, work-hours window and completion log locally.

That is the entire permission list. There is deliberately nothing else.

Not Medical Advice

The 20-20-20 guidance and the posture and hydration intervals are general ergonomic conventions, not medical advice, and this extension does not diagnose or treat anything. If you have symptoms, talk to a professional.

Data Deletion

Everything can be removed at any time with "Reset all" in the popup, or by uninstalling the extension. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.
