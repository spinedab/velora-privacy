Privacy Policy - Velora Invoice

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Overview

Velora Invoice runs entirely inside your browser. It has no server, no account system and no analytics. It makes no network requests at all.

Data collection

None. The extension does not collect, transmit or sell personal data, browsing history, page content or usage statistics. There is no telemetry and no crash reporting.

What is stored, and where

It stores your business details, clients, invoices and quotes.

Everything is kept in chrome.storage.local on your own device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it, and the panel offers a way to erase it on demand.

Network

It makes no network requests.

The manifest declares no host permissions, so the extension cannot contact any server.

Permissions explained

  • sidePanel - The entire interface is a Chrome side panel.
  • storage - Saves your data locally on your device, in chrome.storage.local.
  • downloads - Saves the files you export.

Page access

The extension never reads or modifies the pages you visit.

Data deletion

Use the delete control in the panel, or uninstall the extension. Both remove every stored value immediately.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published with the extension and reflected in the date above.
