Privacy Policy - Velora Repo Ask

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Overview

Velora Repo Ask runs entirely inside your browser. It has no server, no account system and no analytics. The only network request it can make is described below.

Data collection

None. The extension does not collect, transmit or sell personal data, browsing history, page content or usage statistics. There is no telemetry and no crash reporting.

What is stored, and where

It stores your per-repo questions and answers and your preferences on this device. Your Groq key stays in Velora Keys.

Everything is kept in chrome.storage.local on your own device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it, and the panel offers a way to erase it on demand.

Network

Reading the page needs no network. Only when you ask a question does it send context to api.groq.com with your own key.

The extension's manifest permits requests only to: https://api.groq.com/*. No other host can be contacted.

Permissions explained

  • sidePanel - The entire interface is a Chrome side panel.
  • storage - Saves your data locally on your device, in chrome.storage.local.
  • activeTab - Reads the page in the tab you have open, only after you click the toolbar icon.
  • scripting - Runs a read-only script in the current tab to extract content. Nothing is modified.
  • clipboardWrite - Copies results to the clipboard when you press a Copy button.

Page access

The extension never reads or modifies the pages you visit.

Data deletion

Use the delete control in the panel, or uninstall the extension. Both remove every stored value immediately.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published with the extension and reflected in the date above.
