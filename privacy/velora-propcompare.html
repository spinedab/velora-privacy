Privacy Policy - Velora PropCompare

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Velora PropCompare is a local-only Chrome extension that builds a private side-by-side comparison of the properties you are browsing. You add the page you are on, fill in the numbers that matter to you, and the panel computes price per area and monthly cost so you can compare options in one table. It is designed with a privacy-first architecture.

Data Collection

Velora PropCompare does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on external servers. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no telemetry transmitted outside of the browser.

How Data Is Handled

  • No network requests of any kind. This extension has no host permissions and makes no fetch, XHR, WebSocket or beacon calls. There is no AI, no API key and no server component. All arithmetic runs on your device.
  • Local storage only. Listings (address, name, price, area, rooms, fees, commute, rating, shortlist, pros, cons, notes) and your display preference are stored exclusively in chrome.storage.local on the user's device.
  • What is read from a page, and nothing more. When you click "Add this listing", one read-only function returns exactly location.href and document.title. When you click "Read selection to auto-fill", one read-only function returns the text you selected yourself. The extension never queries the listing's DOM, never reads fields you did not select, and never walks the page looking for data.
  • Auto-fill is a typing shortcut, not a scraper. Whatever the parser recognises in your selection is offered in the form for you to confirm or correct before saving. Nothing is stored until you click Save.
  • No page interaction. The extension never clicks, submits, fills forms, navigates, contacts an agent, or modifies the page in any way.
  • Reads are always manual. Nothing is read in the background and no other tab is accessed.
  • User-controlled exports. CSV and JSON files are produced only when you click Export.
  • No user accounts. The extension does not require or support user accounts, login, or registration of any kind.

Page Access

Velora PropCompare declares no persistent content scripts and no host permissions at all — it does not name Zillow, Redfin, Idealista or any other portal. It uses the activeTab permission, which grants temporary access to the current tab only after an explicit user gesture, together with scripting to read the page's address and title, or your own text selection, at that moment.

Permissions Explained

  • sidePanel - renders the comparison workspace in Chrome's side panel.
  • storage - saves your listings and display preference locally.
  • activeTab - temporary access to the current page, only after you click Add or Read selection.
  • scripting - reads the page's address and title, or your text selection, at that moment.
  • downloads - saves the CSV or JSON file when you click Export.

Not Financial Advice

The figures the panel computes (price per area, monthly cost, averages) are arithmetic on numbers you entered. They are a convenience for comparing options, not a valuation, an appraisal, or financial advice.

Data Deletion

All data can be removed at any time by deleting individual listings inside the panel, using "Delete all local data" in the Data tab, or uninstalling the extension. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.
