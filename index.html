Velora Suite

Local-first tools for the browser and the phone. No accounts, no servers of our own, no tracking. Each extension's privacy policy is below.

Velora ClipsMark moments while you watch YouTube, organize them into clip collections, and export them - all local, no cloud, no account.
FewFeed WorkspaceManual content planner for Facebook groups and pages. Draft posts with AI, schedule reminders locally, and insert text by hand.
Velora InboxSide panel copilot for Messenger: snippets, AI drafts with your own key, and human review on every message. You always click Send.
Velora TubeLocal side panel for YouTube creators. Draft optimized titles, descriptions, and tags with AI. Your API key, your data, all local.
Velora AccessMake any page easier to read: dyslexia-friendly text, contrast, dark mode, big cursor, text scale, reading ruler, read aloud. Local.
Velora AgendaTurn text into calendar events: detect dates in a message, review, edit, and download an .ics with recurrence. Local.
Velora CiteGenCitations from the page you are reading: APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, Harvard and BibTeX, formatted locally. Projects, .bib and .ris.
Velora ClipboardClipboard history you control: capture on demand, search, pin favourites, paste back. Local, never automatic.
Velora Code ReviewLocal side panel assistant for GitHub pull requests. AI review checklist on demand. Reads diffs only. Never comments or merges.
Velora CoinsTrack a crypto portfolio you enter yourself. Local-only holdings, prices on demand, no wallet, no advice.

Velora CronCron builder and explainer: plain-language description, next 10 runs in your zone or UTC, presets, validation.
Velora CSSHover an element to inspect its computed CSS in the side panel: box model, typography, colors, snippet and Tailwind hints.
